# Product Overview — TechFlow SaaS Platform

## Introduction

TechFlow is a cloud-native project management and analytics platform designed for
engineering teams that need deep workflow visibility without sacrificing ease of use.
It integrates seamlessly with GitHub, Jira, and Slack to keep your entire development
lifecycle in one place.

---

## Pricing Tiers

TechFlow offers three pricing tiers to accommodate teams of every size:

| Tier           | Price (per seat/month) | Users     | Storage |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | --------- | ------- |
| **Starter**    | Free                   | Up to 5   | 5 GB    |
| **Professional** | $29                  | Up to 50  | 100 GB  |
| **Enterprise** | Custom pricing         | Unlimited | 1 TB+   |

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

---

## Core Features

### Project & Task Management
- Kanban boards, Gantt charts, and list views
- Custom fields, labels, and priority levels
- Recurring task templates and automated workflows

### Integrations
- **GitHub**: auto-link commits, PRs, and issues to TechFlow tasks
- **Jira**: bi-directional sync so existing Jira projects appear in TechFlow
- **Slack**: receive task updates and stand-up summaries in any channel

### Collaboration
- Real-time editing with conflict resolution
- Inline comments with `@mention` notifications
- Guest access for external stakeholders (Professional and above)

---

## Analytics & Reporting

### Professional Plan
The Professional plan includes:
- Sprint velocity charts
- Burndown / burnup graphs
- Individual contributor reports

### Enterprise Plan
Enterprise customers receive the full analytics suite:
- **Advanced analytics** with configurable KPIs
- **Custom dashboards** shared across the organisation
- **Data export** to CSV, JSON, or direct database connectors (PostgreSQL, BigQuery)
- Executive summary emails on a custom schedule

---

## Security & Compliance

- SSO via SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 (Enterprise)
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with fine-grained permissions
- SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant
- End-to-end encryption for data at rest and in transit
- Audit logs retained for 12 months (Enterprise)
